= Tebako is alive — the thrift-free re-architecture

A fair question reached us recently, and it deserves a straight answer:
*is the project dead?*

It is not. But we understand why it might look that way, so here is an
honest account of where things stand and what we are doing about it.

== Where we have been

Public activity has been quiet. In February 2026, Maxim Samsonov
(https://github.com/maxirmx[maxirmx]), Tebako's lead engineer, stepped
back from the project. We are grateful for everything he built — Tebako
exists because of his work, and the foundation he laid is what the rest
of this post builds on.

Meanwhile, the same kinds of build failures kept coming back: Tebako
stopped building on current macOS, newer openssl gems stopped loading,
and every Homebrew or Xcode update seemed to break something new. If
you hit one of these, you have our sympathy — and our thanks for the
bug reports. They told us clearly where the real problem was.

== The real problem: dependencies that move under us

Tebako's filesystem stack — DwarFS, plus its folly and fbthrift
dependencies — has been compiled from source on *your* machine, against
whatever Boost, OpenSSL and toolchain versions happened to be installed
there. When any of those moves, the build breaks, and the failure lands
on someone who just wanted to package an application. There was nothing
wrong with those users' machines, and there was nothing they could do
locally to fix it.

The same design is also why `tebako setup` takes hours: it compiles
everything from source — Ruby, DwarFS, and everything below them —
before the first package is ever produced.

Patching these breaks one at a time would mean asking you to wait
through the same failures again and again. So instead, we are removing
the cause.

== The fix: remove the problem, then prebuild the rest

The re-architecture now in flight (the v0.15 line) does three things:

* *folly and fbthrift are removed entirely.*
  https://github.com/tamatebako/dwarfs-t[dwarfs-t], Tebako's variant
  fork of DwarFS, now follows upstream DwarFS v0.10+ and uses
  FlatBuffers metadata, so Thrift is no longer needed to build or to
  read images. https://github.com/tamatebako/libtfs[libtfs] (renamed
  from libdwarfs), the filesystem layer Tebako links, is folly-free,
  and CI fails the build if folly ever comes back. The dependency
  surface shrinks accordingly.
* *Heavy components ship prebuilt.* libtfs publishes per-platform
  packages, and
  https://github.com/tamatebako/tebako-runtime-ruby[tebako-runtime-ruby]
  publishes prebuilt, tebako-patched Ruby runtimes. `tebako press`
  downloads these components instead of compiling them — no C++
  toolchain on your machine, no hours-long setup, and no dependence on
  the versions your package manager happens to have today.
* *The packaging model gets composable.* Every Tebako binary becomes a
  composition of three parts — a small bootstrap launcher, a language
  runtime, and one or more filesystem images — with tooling to bundle,
  unbundle and reassemble them, and a shared machine-local runtime
  cache under `~/.tebako`, so lean binaries download a runtime once and
  share it across every Tebako application on the machine.

The full design — what is current, what is in flight, and what is
roadmap — is written up on the new link:/architecture/[architecture
page].

== What this means for you today

* The current release line is v0.14.x, and the work above is *in
  flight*, not shipped. We do not promise dates; each piece is released
  when its platform matrix (glibc, musl, macOS arm64/x86_64, Windows
  msys) is green.
* Existing Tebako-packaged binaries and images keep working: the new
  engine keeps read compatibility with thrift-era images, and the
  classic bundle mode remains fully supported.
* The fix for the recurring breakage is not another pin-and-patch
  round — it is the removal of the dependency chain that keeps causing
  it.

If you rely on Tebako: thank you for your patience, and please keep the
bug reports coming — they help. If you are evaluating Tebako: the
link:/architecture/[architecture page] shows where the project is and
where it is going.

**__Press on__** _with Tebako_!
